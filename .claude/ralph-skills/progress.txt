**Learnings:**
- Volatility calculation: use np.diff(closes) / closes[:-1] for returns
- Annualized volatility = daily_vol * sqrt(252)
- Position limit mapping: low_vol(<15%) -> 25%, med_vol(15-30%) -> 12.5-20%, high_vol(30-50%) -> 5-15%, very_high(>50%) -> 10%
- Correlation multiplier: high corr (>=0.8) -> 0.7x, neutral (0.4-0.6) -> 1.0x, low corr (<0.2) -> 1.10x
